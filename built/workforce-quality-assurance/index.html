Workforce quality assurance pipeline

2025 · llms · dbt · snowflake · python

The primary quality assurance system for a large provider workforce. It runs against the whole population, not a sample. When something goes wrong, an AI investigator reads the provider’s full relationship history and writes up the evidence. Issues become flags, three flags trigger an automated strike, and a person reviews it before it stands.

I designed and built it and still own the decision logic, the investigator prompts and the exemptions, through three major versions.
