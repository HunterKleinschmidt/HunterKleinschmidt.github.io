bio

I build things with AI and write about what I learn. I lead analytics and AI systems at Solace Health, one of the fastest-growing companies in America, where I built our data layer and the AI systems the company runs on.

My background is in academia, where I did graduate research for NASA’s Europa Clipper mission. Then I left to build, and started and led my own company for three years.

I build things with AI and write about what I learn.

I spent most of my early career in academia. My dream was to work for NASA, and as a PhD student at Arizona State University I got to do graduate research on the Europa Clipper mission, on the MASPEX team. I sat in rooms with the scientists running the mission, which still feels a little unreal.

But I wanted to get out and build. So I left and started my own company, Klein Training App: a mobile app that gave athletes personalized strength and conditioning programs. I ran it for three years.

Then I joined Solace Health, right after its Series B, on a small data team running dashboards on a raw warehouse. I’ve helped grow it into an AI-native function since, building the data layer, the AI systems the company runs its quality assurance and billing integrity on, and a context layer that gives every analyst’s AI the team’s knowledge.

I think those three pieces are the whole story: a scientist’s habit of testing everything, a founder’s scrappiness, and what I’ve learned helping a company scale. Data is how I got here, not where I’m stopping.

What I want is simple to say and hard to do: work on the hardest problems with real stakes, alongside the best problem solvers I can find.
